Bloom is a personal iPhone nutrition and wellness diary. Log meals by camera or photo library, review estimated calories and macros, track water, weight and waist, and keep daily routines and exercise records.

Optional Apple Health access reads selected daily summaries. Optional Google sign-in saves encrypted recovery archives and readable meal photos and tracking spreadsheets to your own Google Drive.

Bloom is a privately installed app. This information site does not offer public accounts or download your diary. The app source and personal data are private.

Your data, your account

Google access is limited to files used with the app through the drive.file scope. Connecting Google alone does not upload your diary; backup/export settings control uploads. Read our privacy information and usage terms.

Photo estimates require your configured AI service. In direct iPhone mode, the selected meal image is sent to OpenAI using your own API key. Nutrition estimates and the body visualization are approximate.
