Privacy

This describes the current personal-use Bloom iPhone app. This static information site has no sign-in, analytics script, diary database or upload form. Its hosting provider may process ordinary access logs under its own policies.

On the iPhone

The app stores your diary and compressed meal photos in its local container. Entries can include meal nutrition, portions, capture date/time and timezone, weight, waist, water, notes, supplement or medication routines and exercise records. API keys, Google sessions and the backup recovery key are stored separately using iOS secure storage. The widget shares local calorie/macro totals and targets, the latest recorded weight/date, date/timezone, display preference and minimal scan status (identifier, timestamps, count and a completed meal name/calories) with the app extension. Photos, Health readings and credentials are not copied into that shared widget snapshot.

Optional Google connection

Google Sign-In identifies your chosen account and requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file access to files used with Bloom. Bloom uses that connection to create/update encrypted recovery archives, optional readable meal-photo copies and a tracking Google Sheet in your Drive. It can list/download its backups for recovery and check its managed files and spreadsheet tables. It does not request unrestricted access to your entire Drive.

Readable exports can contain meal calories/macros, portions, photos and timestamps, body measurements and notes, water, routines, exercise records and selected Health summaries if explicitly enabled. People you share these Google files with can read those exports. Encrypted archives require the separately saved recovery key. The app does not upload Google tokens or your AI API key into the archive or Sheet.

Google user data is used only to provide these user-requested backup/export/restore features. It is not sold, used for advertising or transferred for unrelated purposes, and it is not used to train generalized AI models. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Photo analysis and Health

When you submit a meal photo, the selected compressed image and optional portion notes are sent to the AI endpoint you configured. Direct iPhone mode uses OpenAI, whose API policies and your account settings govern processing there. This is separate from Google backup. Apple Health sharing is optional and read-only; only selected daily summaries are retained/exported when you enable that option. Bloom does not overwrite Apple Health.

Retention and control

You can delete diary entries, disable uploads, sign out or revoke Google's access in the app or your Google Account. Signing out does not delete existing files. Deleting a meal updates the current backup/Sheet after successful sync but old archives and separate readable photo copies can retain earlier data. Remove those files through Google Drive when no longer wanted. Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app; keep a verified backup and recovery key first. iOS controls background timing, so check actual backup completion times.

No app-owner server receives your diary in direct iPhone mode. If you choose the optional self-hosted backend, its operator and configuration govern that processing. Contact the app owner through the linked GitHub profile for questions about this personal installation.
